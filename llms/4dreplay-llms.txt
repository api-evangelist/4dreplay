# 4DReplay

> 4DReplay is a media technology company that builds multi-camera, time-slice ("bullet-time") video capture and replay systems for live sports, entertainment and events. Arrays of roughly 50 to 120 synchronized cameras — locked to within 1/10,000 of a second — are processed in real time to produce any-angle replay highlights within seconds of a play and deliver them to broadcast, mobile and OTT audiences. Founded in 2012 by Hongsu Jung; headquartered in San Mateo, California with offices in Seoul and Tokyo.

## Products

- [4DReplay](https://4dreplay.com/): Premium broadcast instant replay — frame-accurate multi-camera capture and any-angle replay for professional leagues and broadcasters.
- 4DReplay+ / 4DLive: Broadcast replay with live overlays (speed, angle, tracking data), automated production, and 5G-based interactive multi-view live streaming.
- [4Dist](https://4dist.com/): Browser-based interactive OTT platform — multi-angle replay plus automated AI motion, swing and pitching analysis, with operator / coach / player roles.
